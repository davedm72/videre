## 🚀 What is videre?

videre is a powerful, free application that helps you take control of your photos and videos. Do you have folders full of photos with names like "IMG_4821.jpg" that mean nothing to you? Are you tired of scrolling through hundreds of images to find that one vacation photo? videre solves these problems by:

- **Location sorting** – Organizes photos by where they were taken using built-in GPS data

## 📖 How to Use videre

### First-Time Setup

When you first open videre, you'll see a welcome screen. Click "Get Started" to begin. The application will ask you to select folders where your photos and videos are stored. You can choose multiple folders (like your Pictures folder, Desktop, or external drives).

### Main Features Explained

#### 🔍 Smart Search
Type a description of what you're looking for in the search bar. For example, try "red car" or "family at beach." videre will show you matching photos and videos instantly.

#### 👥 Face Groups
videre automatically finds faces in your photos and groups them together. Click on a face group to see all photos of that person. You can name the groups to make them easier to find later.

#### 🗑️ Duplicate Cleaner
Go to the "Duplicates" section to see groups of identical or very similar photos. Review them and select which ones to keep. videre will safely move duplicates to your Recycle Bin.

#### 📍 Location View
If your photos have location data, you'll see them on a map view. This makes it easy to relive trips and see where each photo was taken.

#### 🏷️ Auto-Tagging
videre automatically adds tags to your files based on their content. These tags appear in Windows File Explorer, making your files searchable even outside videre.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Tips

### Application Won't Start
- Make sure your Windows is up to date
- Try right-clicking the videre icon and selecting "Run as administrator"
- Check if your antivirus software is blocking the application

### Photos Not Appearing
- Verify you selected the correct folders during setup
- Ensure your photos are in common formats like JPG, PNG, or HEIC
- Try clicking "Refresh" in the top menu

### Slow Performance
- Close other programs while using videre
- Try processing smaller batches of photos at once
- Check that you have enough free disk space
